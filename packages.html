WHAT TO EXPECT ON THE DAY

Tour Program & Itinerary

A typical evening safari sequence designed for maximum comfort, thrill, and memorable moments.

1
03:00 PM – 03:30 PM

4x4 SUV Hotel Pickup

Your licensed safari driver arrives at your hotel reception in a clean, climate-controlled Toyota Land Cruiser 4x4. Enjoy a comfortable ride to the Lahbab Red Dunes.

2
04:15 PM – 05:00 PM

Red Dune Bashing & Sandboarding

Tires deflated, seatbelts on! Experience thrilling 4x4 dune bashing across steep red dunes, followed by sandboarding down high sand slopes.

3
05:15 PM – 05:45 PM

Golden Hour Sunset Photography

Stop at a high dune peak for panoramic 360-degree desert sunset views. Perfect lighting for family, couple, and portrait photos.

4
06:00 PM – 07:30 PM

Bedouin Camp Arrival & Arabian Hospitality

Warm Arabic coffee, dates & drinks upon arrival. Enjoy camel rides, traditional henna tattoo painting, and shisha lounge relaxation.

5
07:30 PM – 09:00 PM

5-Star BBQ Feast & Live Shows

Savor an elaborate BBQ buffet featuring grilled kebabs, chicken tikka, vegetarian specialties, and Arabic sweets alongside Tanoura, Fire, and Belly Dance shows.

6
09:30 PM – 10:00 PM

Return Drop-Off to Your Hotel

Relax in comfort as your safari driver returns you directly to your hotel or apartment doorstep in Dubai.
