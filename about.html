About Red Camel Desert Safari

We operate our own fleet of 4x4 Toyota Land Cruisers, private Bedouin desert camp, and certified desert drivers—delivering authentic Dubai adventures without middleman commissions.

OUR HERITAGE & MISSION

Delivering Authentic Arabian Hospitality

Founded over 15 years ago, Red Camel Tourism has grown to become one of Dubai’s most trusted and highly-rated desert safari operators. Fully licensed by the Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DET License No. 1274693), our primary focus has always been safety, authenticity, and transparent pricing.

Unlike online travel agencies and ticket resellers that add 25–40% markup fees, Red Camel handles your entire journey in-house—from hotel pickup in our luxury 4x4 fleet to our custom desert camp in the heart of the Lahbab Red Dunes.
